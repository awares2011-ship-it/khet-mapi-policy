Privacy Policy – Khet Mapi (Land Measurement)

Effective date: 22 September 2026 · Package: com.khetmapi.landmeasurement

Khet Mapi ("we", "our", "the app") helps farmers measure farmland using GPS. This policy explains what data the app uses. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal information.

1. Location data

  • The app uses your device location (GPS) only while you measure a field, and only after you grant permission.
  • Location is processed on your device to compute area and distance. We never transmit your location to our servers — we operate no servers.
  • You can revoke location permission anytime in Android Settings; measurement features will then show a message asking you to turn location back on.

2. Data stored on your device

Fields you save (name, area, boundary points, date) are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Clearing app data (Settings → Clear My Data) deletes them too.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob to keep the app free. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID and device identifiers
  • Approximate location (for ad relevance), app interactions and crash/diagnostic data

This collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data practices, not by us. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

4. Map tiles

The satellite/plain map loads map image tiles from third-party providers (Esri World Imagery, OpenStreetMap contributors) over the internet. Standard technical logs (e.g. IP address) may be visible to those providers per their policies.

5. No accounts, no tracking by us

The app requires no sign-up, collects no name, phone number, email or contacts, and contains no analytics SDK that reports to us.

6. Permissions used

  • Location (fine/coarse) – field measurement.
  • Internet – map tiles and ads.

7. Children

The app is a general farming utility not directed at children under 13, and we knowingly collect no data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new effective date.
